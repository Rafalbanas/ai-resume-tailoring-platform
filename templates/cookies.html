This application uses first-party cookies to keep you signed in and protect forms and other requests. These cookies are necessary to provide the service; they are not used for advertising or audience tracking, so we do not ask for consent to set them.

Analytics and advertising

The application does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Posting a link to this application on LinkedIn does not add a LinkedIn tracker to it.

Your browser settings

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking these essential cookies can prevent sign-in and form submissions from working. On a shared device, sign out and clear this site's cookies after use.

Scope of this notice

This notice describes browser cookies set by CVTailor. It does not cover how submitted job offers, profile data or generated documents are processed. Essential cookies do not require a consent choice, but other processing of personal data still requires appropriate privacy information.
