# Qualification coverage and source-bound editorial checks

Explicit Polish experience, understanding, communication and named-language
conditions must be assessed as qualifications, even when a model classifies
them as duties or context. Bare technology tags and action duties are not made
mandatory merely by their section heading. Existing saved analyses with these
omissions fail coverage revalidation rather than silently becoming complete.

Language checks retain the employer's literal level. A B2 model label or portal
tag cannot satisfy an advanced/C1 employer condition. Polish language names and
the explicit advanced-level qualifier are recognized by the evidence matcher.

A general Computer Science degree condition can be recovered from a documented
degree record rather than a model's unsupported claim that the degree is ongoing.
The recovery uses the matching education record's citation, excludes pending or
future-dated degrees, and does not generalize to specialist degrees, doctorates,
grades or combined experience requirements.

Summary composition selects complete attested project clauses rather than two
sentences about the same pipeline/model-evaluation topic within one project.
Explicit MSc-thesis provenance is preferred to a redundant generic project
description. No project is promoted to commercial employment; existing summary
confirmation requirements remain unchanged.

Optional headline suffixes share a 64-character total budget, both for accepted
model-selected skill directions and the existing advertised-title fallback.
Only whole eligible skill labels fit: first two, then one, then none. The suffix
budget does not shorten the real occupation, strip basic/academic qualifiers or
turn a target job title into employment. Existing headline schema limits remain
unchanged; this is not a blanket guarantee of a particular number of PDF lines.

Ticketing declarations remain available as corroboration but do not establish
practical ticket ownership. Git use does not establish advanced proficiency.
Provider recovery also resolves rejected canonical labels through literal source
quotes, retrying only affected passages within the existing repair-call bound.
Irrelevant evidence and unsupported confirmations remain validation failures if
the model cannot repair them. These safeguards do not guarantee successful
analysis of every offer or eliminate the need for owner review.

Changes apply to future processing. Existing production CVs, draft approvals,
profile facts, quotas and provider settings are not rewritten or changed.
