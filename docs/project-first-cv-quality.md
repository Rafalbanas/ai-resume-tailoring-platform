# Source-grounded, project-focused CV content

New drafts prioritize documented technical evidence for the advertised role,
without turning a desired title into an occupation or project work into employment.

## Presentation and selection

- Target-title families cover AI engineering, analyst and architect roles,
  Polish AI engineer titles, automation, software and forward-deployed engineering.
  Data engineering/science retain their separate relevance rules. A support title
  remains support-first even if its description mentions AI tools.
- Source-composed summaries retain the actual occupation, followed by relevant
  project facts rather than generic support details. Project and academic scope
  labels and source IDs remain attached. Whole attested clauses are selected;
  sentences are not sliced to fit a character budget.
- Eligible technical skills rank before generic support fillers for these roles.
  Actual basic levels, learning exclusions and manually selected skill membership
  are preserved. No missing tool is added by matching a job advertisement.

## Qualification coverage and scope

Polish minimum-tenure wording (including `2-letnie` / `2 letnie` and `Masz co
najmniej ...`) cannot disappear into context. English second-person capability
statements under a qualification heading and labelled `Deep understanding` /
`Demonstrated ability` clauses must also be assessed, not silently discarded.

A context-only disposition of these conditions fails source validation and needs
analysis recovery or `RETRY`; it cannot establish `APPLY`. Existing bounded
recovery and provider/fallback consent rules are unchanged, with no new model call.

Elapsed employment years alone do not prove an unspecified "similar role".
Such equivalence requires review. Generic IT duties and a personal project do not
establish large-organization experience or foundational platform ownership.
Neither title prestige nor omission of a numeric years requirement proves role fit.

## Boundaries and checks

These bounded lexical guards are not a universal parser or competence assessment.
They do not add cloud, agent-framework or vector-database qualifications, raise
language levels, change job titles, or guarantee hiring outcomes. Existing saved
analyses, CVs and manual edits are not automatically rewritten.
